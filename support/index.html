WorkoutLog Support

Help with accounts, workout history and WorkoutLog Premium.

Contact support

Email mertkizilogluu@hotmail.com. Include your app version and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords or payment-card information.

Restore Premium

Sign in to the WorkoutLog account used for the purchase, open the Premium screen and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account that made the purchase.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are billed and managed by the store. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your Apple Account, then Subscriptions. Deleting a WorkoutLog account does not cancel store billing.

Sync workout history

Cloud history requires a WorkoutLog account and an internet connection. Guest workouts stay on the guest device. If signed-in history does not refresh, confirm you are using the same WorkoutLog account, reconnect to the internet and reopen the app.

Delete an account

In WorkoutLog, open Settings, choose Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps. For deletion assistance when you cannot access the app, contact support from the email associated with the account.
