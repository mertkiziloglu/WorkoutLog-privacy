WorkoutLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026

This policy explains what information WorkoutLog processes and the choices available to you.

Data we collect

When you choose to create an account, WorkoutLog receives your account identifier and sign-in provider through Supabase. Depending on the provider and the privacy choices you make with Apple or Google, it may also receive an email address, display name or profile image. Completed workout history is stored both on your device and in your private Supabase account so it can be restored on another device. This history can include workout dates and duration, exercises, sets, weight, repetitions, hold times, workout notes, body weight attached to a session, and daily check-in information such as mood, sleep, nutrition and cardio. Guest workouts remain only on the guest device. Subscription status and product identifiers are processed by Apple, Google and RevenueCat; WorkoutLog does not receive your payment card details.

How we use data

We use account data to authenticate you, show your profile, link Premium access to the same account across your devices, restore purchases, synchronize and back up your completed workout history, prevent fraud and provide support. RevenueCat also uses the account identifier and purchase history to provide customer history and aggregate subscription analytics. We do not sell personal data or use it for cross-app advertising.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, account storage and private workout-history synchronization. Apple and Google provide optional sign-in. RevenueCat, Apple App Store and Google Play process subscription and purchase status. We require our service providers to process information only for the services described here and to apply protections consistent with this policy.

Retention and security

Account, subscription and synchronized workout-history records are retained while your account exists. Workout history remains available locally for offline use. Deletion removes account-owned cloud records from active systems promptly; limited security logs or protected backups may remain only as long as required for backup integrity, fraud prevention or law. Network traffic uses encrypted connections and cloud workout rows are restricted to the authenticated account. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and account deletion

You may use the app offline as a guest. A signed-in user can permanently delete their WorkoutLog account from Settings. Deletion removes the account, profile, synchronized cloud workout history, mirrored subscription record, RevenueCat customer record and locally stored workout/profile data.

Deleting the account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; billing continues until it is cancelled in the relevant store settings.

Children

WorkoutLog is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
