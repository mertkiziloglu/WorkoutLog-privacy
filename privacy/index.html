WorkoutLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026

This policy explains what information WorkoutLog processes and the choices available to you.

Data we collect

When you choose to create an account, WorkoutLog receives your account identifier, email address, display name, profile image and sign-in provider from Google, Apple and Supabase. Workout entries and body statistics are stored on your device. Subscription status and product identifiers are processed by Apple, Google and RevenueCat; WorkoutLog does not receive your payment card details.

How we use data

We use account data to authenticate you, show your profile, link Premium access to the same account across your devices, restore purchases, prevent fraud and provide support. We do not sell personal data or use it for cross-app advertising.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication and account storage. Google and Apple provide optional sign-in. RevenueCat, Apple App Store and Google Play process subscription and purchase status. We require our service providers to process information only for the services described here and to apply protections consistent with this policy.

Retention and security

Account and subscription records are retained while your account exists. Deletion removes them from active systems promptly; limited security logs or protected backups may remain only as long as required for backup integrity, fraud prevention or law. Network traffic uses encrypted connections. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and account deletion

You may use the app offline as a guest. A signed-in user can permanently delete their WorkoutLog account from Settings. Deletion removes the account, profile, mirrored subscription record, RevenueCat customer record and locally stored workout/profile data, and revokes the associated Apple sign-in token when applicable.

Deleting the account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; billing continues until it is cancelled in the relevant store settings.

Children

WorkoutLog is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, contact meroot@me.com.
